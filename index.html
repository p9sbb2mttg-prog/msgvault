msgvault

A private, self-hosted archive for your own email, chat, and meeting data.

What it is

msgvault is a personal archival tool. It connects to your own accounts — Gmail and Google Calendar, plus optional chat and meeting sources — and keeps a local, full-text-searchable copy of that data on hardware you control.

What it does

  • Syncs your own mailbox on a schedule so you keep a durable, portable copy.
  • Indexes messages, attachments, and relationship metadata for local search.
  • Stores everything in a local database on the owner’s own devices.
  • Runs on a private home network / self-hosted server. There is no vendor cloud.

Who it is for

msgvault is a personal-use application operated solely by its owner for their own accounts. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the general public.

Privacy

Your data stays on your hardware and is never sold or shared. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is accessed and how it is handled.

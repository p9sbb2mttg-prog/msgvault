Privacy Policy

Application: msgvault · Effective date: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the msgvault application accesses, how that information is used, and how it is protected. msgvault is a personal archival tool operated by its owner for their own accounts on their own hardware.

1. Who this applies to

msgvault is a private, self-hosted application. It is used by a single operator to archive that operator’s own email, calendar, and related data. It is not a public or commercial service, and it does not collect data from anyone other than its operator.

2. Information msgvault accesses

When you connect a Google account, msgvault requests access only to the following:

  • Gmail — read access (gmail.readonly): to read messages, headers, labels, and attachments in your own mailbox.
  • Gmail — modify access (gmail.modify): to maintain archive state (for example, applying labels used to track sync progress). msgvault does not send, delete, or alter the content of your messages.

Only data belonging to the account that grants consent is accessed. No other user’s data is read.

3. How the information is used

  • To create and maintain a local copy of your own mailbox for backup and archival.
  • To build a local full-text search index over that data.
  • To produce local analytics (for example, message counts over time) for the operator’s own use.

The data is used exclusively for these archival and personal-search purposes.

4. Storage and security

  • All archived data is stored in a local database on hardware controlled by the operator (a personal computer and a private home server).
  • There is no msgvault cloud service and no third-party storage provider.
  • OAuth credentials are stored with owner-only file permissions (0600) and are transmitted only over encrypted connections (TLS) or the operator’s private network.
  • Access to the local archive requires access to the operator’s own devices.

5. Sharing and disclosure

msgvault does not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party. Your data is not used for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, or any purpose beyond the archival and search functions described above. No human other than the operator reads the archived content.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure. msgvault’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Data is retained for as long as the operator keeps the local archive.
  • The operator can delete the archive at any time by removing the local database and its associated files.
  • You can revoke msgvault’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further syncing immediately.

7. Children

msgvault is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application or in applicable requirements. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your data is handled may be sent to querry.raobelina@gmail.com.
